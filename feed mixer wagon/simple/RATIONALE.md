# Feed mixer wagon - rationale

## Small groups, many trips

Mixing takes power: tractor vertical mixers use roughly 5 kW per m3 of feed. With a 2 kW motor and a 1.9 kWh
battery the bin is limited to ~0.3 m3 (~90 kg). That is a ration for ~2 cows, or for 10-12 calves: the wagon suits
calves, young stock, dry cows and small herds, fed several times a day (fresh feed, less waste). A herd of 100 cows
(4.5 t a day) needs a bigger machine.

## Vertical auger in a cone

A conical bin with a conical flight lifts the feed in the middle and lets it fall at the wall: it mixes long silage
without a separate chopper; 2 knives on the flight cut long stems. 30 rpm keeps the torque at ~450 Nm, a planetary
gearbox of i = 100 under the floor takes the motor's 3000 rpm.

## Where it sits

The bin's centre is at y -50: at the height of the steering motors (z 1032) its radius is 360 mm, well clear of the
stacks at x 295-455, y 405-595, and the GNSS dome at y 500-650 keeps most of its sky. The load cells stand on 2 cross
tubes in the upper-beam grid holes (y -375, 275), placed between the bolt heads (x +-240).

## Door along the cone

The bin wall leans out by 10.5 degrees. A door sliding straight up outside the opening would run into the wall, so
the door, its guides and its actuator are drawn upright and turned onto the wall: the door slides up along the cone.
The chute below is a fixed slope to x 720, so the feed lands beside the robot in the feed alley.

## Estimated, not measured

- Feed density 330 kg/m3 and the usable fill (85 %)
- Mixing power 5 kW/m3, mixing time 6 min, discharge 3 min
- Drive power with a full bin (300 W), trip time 6 min
- Ration per cow 45 kg and per calf 8 kg fresh TMR
- Motor (11 kg), gearbox (14 kg), load cell and actuator masses
