# Disc brushes for the Bruut

Electrically driven disc brushes behind the standard Bruut OpenAgbot, for loose material on concrete or a paved yard
(sawdust, sand, feed remains, dry manure, leaves).

- **2 × Ø750 or 2 × Ø650** side by side: turn the fronts **inwards** to make a windrow in the middle, or
  **outwards** to spread a windrow or heap wide. Only the direction of the motors changes.
- **1 × Ø850**: sweeps the robot's own width (850 mm) to one side.
- One kit: the same toolbar, the same brush unit and the same 48 V worm gearmotor for every brush. The units slide
  along the toolbar on U-bolts, the height is set in slotted holes with a jack bolt. One 24 mm spanner.
