# Compost spreader / bin

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Spreading & Bedding.

## What it does

Transporting compost within the frame structure and spreading it between the crop rows.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A bin between the beams with a bottom belt or screw that meters compost to drop tubes between the rows. Heavy payload, so axle load and the low centre of gravity matter more than for the other spreaders.

## Reuse from existing implements

Metering of the straw and sawdust spreaders; axle-load calculation of the *_calc.py files.

## Open questions

- Payload versus wheel and motor ratings
- Bin shape for flow of damp compost
- Metering accuracy and bridging of the material
