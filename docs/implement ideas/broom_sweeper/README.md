# Broom / sweeper

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Transport & General.

## What it does

Large front-mounted brush or electrically driven radial brush for surface cleaning, as an alternative to the front push blade.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A cylindrical (radial) brush across the front with a 48 V motor and height adjustment. The disc brushes already exist for the rear; a roller brush at the front sweeps material sideways or into a collector.

## Reuse from existing implements

Disc brushes (brushes/simple): motors, toolbar and mounting; push blade as the alternative.

## Open questions

- Radial brush diameter, length and bristle type
- Sweep direction and collector
- Height and pressure control
- Dust control
