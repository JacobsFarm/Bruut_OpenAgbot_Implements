# Tow hitch

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Transport & General.

## What it does

Rear-mounted hitch for pulling trailers and other rolling equipment.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A drawbar plate bolted to the rear flange holes of the wheel brackets or to the lower beams, with a ball coupling or pin. The powered trailer of the mobile refiller already needs one.

## Reuse from existing implements

Trailer coupling of the mobile refiller (liquid) and the flange mounting of the feed pusher auger.

## Open questions

- Maximum drawbar pull and vertical load
- Coupling type
- Reverse driving with a trailer
