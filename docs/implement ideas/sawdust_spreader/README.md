# Sawdust spreader

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Spreading & Bedding.

## What it does

Even distribution of sawdust or bedding material in stables.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A bin on the robot with a bottom belt or screw and a throwing wheel or distributor, so a stable can be bedded without a tractor. Hopper height must clear the stable doors; weight is balanced over the axles.

## Reuse from existing implements

Auger and hood design of the feed pusher auger; mobile refiller for reloading.

## Open questions

- Bin size versus door height and axle load
- Throwing width and evenness
- Dust and moisture of the material
- Stable floor and slope
