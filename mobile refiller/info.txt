This folder contains the mobile refiller: a support robot that tops up the working robots in the field (liquid,
seed, granular fertilizer, water, batteries, ...). See README.md (concept and all uses), liquid/ (FreeCAD model,
calculations and the animation of a refill in the turn) and simple/ (standard IBC with a hanging hose; the working
robot skips passes and is topped up in the middle of its U-turn). stationary/ holds refill stations that stand on
the headland (IBC trailer for 1 or 2 IBCs, three-point frame, standard flat wagon), each with its own control box
(pump, motor, electronics) and an RTK antenna above the nozzle; the working robot drives to them.
