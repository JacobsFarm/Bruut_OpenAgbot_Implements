# (Rubber) push blade / grader blade

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Soil Cultivation & Weed Control.

## What it does

Front-mounted or mid-mounted between the front and rear wheels. Usable as a grader for levelling terrain, for pushing feed and grass back in the stable, or for cleaning silos.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A wide blade with a replaceable rubber or steel wearing edge on a pivoting frame, lifted by a linear actuator like the lift frames of the other implements. A hinge with a float stop lets the blade follow the floor; an angled (dozer) setting moves material to one side. Front-mounted it pushes, mid-mounted it scrapes under the belly.

## Reuse from existing implements

Lift frame and headstock of the liquid fertilizer applicator or overseeder; the push-back job overlaps with the feed pusher auger.

## Open questions

- Blade width: robot width (850 mm) or wider with side wings?
- Push force against wheel grip: how much load can the 4 wheels transmit?
- Rubber edge for concrete versus steel edge for soil
- Is the blade angle adjustable, and by what (actuator or pins)?
