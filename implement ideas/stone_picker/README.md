# Stone picker

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Crop & Soil.

## What it does

Picks stones out of grassland or a seedbed and carries them to a bin, which is then emptied at the field edge.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A shallow comb or tine belt that lifts stones onto a short conveyor into a bin. Works in a pass like the belt rake and is lifted at the headland.

## Reuse from existing implements

Belt, tine bars and castor-actuator lift of the belt rake (bandrake/electric); bin and tipping idea of the dumper.

## Open questions

- Stone size range and tine spacing
- Bin size versus robot axle load
- Soil loss over the belt (sieving)
- Emptying: tip at the headland or hand over to the refiller platform
