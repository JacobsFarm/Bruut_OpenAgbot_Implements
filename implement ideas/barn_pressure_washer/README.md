# Barn pressure washer

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Livestock & Farm.

## What it does

Cleans pens, walls and floors with a high-pressure water jet when the barn is empty, around the clock. Seen on the Envirologic Evo Cleaner (pig barns).

## Concept (first thoughts)

A pressure washer pump on board (water by hose or from a tank) and a nozzle on a small arm with two axes that follows a taught pattern per pen. The robot moves from pen to pen on a stored route.

## Reuse from existing implements

Arm and axes of the weed picker or field marker; tank and pump of the mobile refiller; motor options of the heavy-duty electric motors.

## Open questions

- Water supply: hose reel or tank
- Waterproofing of the robot and its electronics
- Arm reach for walls and partitions
- Navigation in a wet, empty barn
