# Flail mulcher

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Crop & Soil.

## What it does

Shreds prunings, tough grass, stubble and crop residues between rows instead of only cutting them. Seen on orchard and vineyard robots (Slopehelper drum mulcher, XAG RM80, Bonsai OMC AR500).

## Concept (first thoughts)

A rear-mounted flail rotor with Y-knives or hammers in a closed housing with a rear roller that sets the cutting height. Belt-driven from a strong electric motor. A swing-out side section could also reach under the tree row.

## Reuse from existing implements

Housing, belt drive and height setting of the mower unit (mowing deck); motor options of the heavy-duty electric motors; swing-out arm of the around-pole fence mower.

## Open questions

- Power needed for prunings versus grass
- Rotor speed, knife type and stone throw protection
- Width that fits the row spacing
- Mass on the rear and balance with counterweights
