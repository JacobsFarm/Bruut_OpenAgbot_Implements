# Cover crop undersower

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Crop & Soil.

## What it does

Sows a cover crop (green manure) in a crop that is already standing, between the rows.

## Concept (first thoughts)

Narrow slit or broadcast seeding between rows of 375 mm spacing, light enough that the robot's wheels run in the inter-rows. Seed from a small hopper with a speed-following roller.

## Reuse from existing implements

Seed hopper, metering rollers and slit elements of the overseeder; row geometry of the in-row weeder.

## Open questions

- Slit versus broadcast between rows
- Species and seed rate
- Timing relative to crop height (clearance under the belly)
