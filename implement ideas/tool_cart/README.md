# Tool and material cart

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Transport & General.

## What it does

Carries tools, stakes, nets, and water along to where the work is, in the field or in a greenhouse.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A rack or open box on the top plate or a small trailer, with tool holders. Follows the worker or drives to a stored point.

## Reuse from existing implements

Crate carrier platform, tow hitch idea.

## Open questions

- Rack layout and capacity
- Following or fixed route
- Greenhouse use: width and floor
