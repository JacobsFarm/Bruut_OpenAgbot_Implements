# Crate and box carrier

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Transport & General.

## What it does

Follows a picker or runs a set route and carries full crates from the plant rows to the field edge, so pickers do not have to walk with loads.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A flat platform on the top plate with crate guides and tie-downs, taking 4 to 8 standard crates. Follow-me mode via a tag or RTK on the picker, or fixed shuttle routes. Weight is kept low and centred over the axles.

## Reuse from existing implements

Top-plate pattern and the mobile refiller's driving between two positions; speaker idea for warnings and messages.

## Open questions

- Crate type and number, payload
- Follow-me method: RTK tag, camera or radio
- Loading height for the picker
- Fruit bruising on bumpy ground
