# Bag trailer

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Transport & General.

## What it does

Pulls a small trailer with bags of seed, fertilizer or feed to a fixed point in the field or yard.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A light single-axle trailer or a pallet on a platform, hitched to the tow hitch. Supplies refill stations or spreaders.

## Reuse from existing implements

Trailer of the mobile refiller; tow hitch idea.

## Open questions

- Load per trip and slope
- Hitch type and reverse driving
- Unloading: by hand or tipped
