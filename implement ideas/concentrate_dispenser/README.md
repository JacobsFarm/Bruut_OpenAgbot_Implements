# Concentrate dispenser on the feed table

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Livestock & Farm.

## What it does

Doses concentrates or minerals on the feed along the fence while pushing feed, to draw cows to the fence. Seen on the Euromilk EM Lizard and Schauer Faro.

## Concept (first thoughts)

One or two small hoppers on the feed pusher with a metering auger driven by a stepper, dropping a thin line on the pushed feed. Dose per metre or per feeding place is set in the route.

## Reuse from existing implements

Feed pusher auger (carrier and route); metering auger and hopper of the overseeder or fertilizer spreader.

## Open questions

- Hopper size and filling
- Dose accuracy per metre at the driving speed
- Moisture and caking of the concentrate
- Link with the herd management system
