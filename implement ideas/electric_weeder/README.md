# Electric weed control

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Weed & Crop Protection.

## What it does

Applies a high-voltage current through weeds with an electrode, between rows or in the row, to kill the plant including its root.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A copper or steel electrode bar just above the weeds with an earth return through a wheel, fed by a high-voltage converter from the 48 V bus. Mounted behind or in front, lifted by an actuator.

## Reuse from existing implements

Lift frame of the applicator; control box of the weeds burner.

## Open questions

- Voltage, power and speed for a given weed size
- Operator and animal safety near the electrode
- Earth return through the wheels or a skid
- Crop contact: only between rows or also in the row
