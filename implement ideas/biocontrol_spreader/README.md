# Biological-control spreader

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Weed & Crop Protection.

## What it does

Releases beneficial organisms (parasitic wasps, predatory mites, nematodes) at set points or lines in the crop.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A small cooled container with a dosing unit that drops carriers at GPS points or along rows. For nematodes a small tank with a pump and spray nozzle; for insects a dispenser with a drop tube.

## Reuse from existing implements

Dosing pump and tank of the liquid fertilizer applicator and spot sprayer; GPS point handling of the field marker.

## Open questions

- Organism type, carrier and storage temperature
- Dosing per point or per metre
- Survival during dispensing
- Timing and weather limits
