# Battery pack

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Power & Drive.

## What it does

Additional power supply modules to be installed between the frames.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A removable battery module in a protective case, bolted between the beams on the 50 mm grid, connected to the 48 V bus through a fuse and contactor. Fits as ballast too, so it can double as a counterweight.

## Reuse from existing implements

Control-box mounting of the mobile refiller and the weeds burner.

## Open questions

- Cell format, capacity and mass
- Connector and fuse rating
- Battery management and charging
- Quick-swap mechanism
