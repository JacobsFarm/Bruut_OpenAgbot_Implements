# Insect vacuum / beetle catcher

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Weed & Crop Protection.

## What it does

Sucks or knocks pest insects off the crop instead of spraying: lygus bugs in strawberries, Colorado beetles and larvae in potatoes. Seen on the Agrobot Bug Vacuum and the Fieldworkers Colorado beetle catcher.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A hood over one or two rows with an air flow that pulls the insects off the plants into a catch box with a fine screen (or a fan that kills them), with flexible strips that shake the plants. For beetles: brushes or beaters that knock them into a tray below the canopy.

## Reuse from existing implements

Fan, hose and bin of the debris vacuum; row hood and strip curtains of the potato disease scout.

## Open questions

- Air speed that lifts the insects without damaging the crop or flowers
- Catch box, killing method and emptying
- Fan power and battery use per hectare
- Effect on beneficial insects
