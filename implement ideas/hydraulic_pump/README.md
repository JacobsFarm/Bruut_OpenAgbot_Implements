# Hydraulic pump

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Transport & General.

## What it does

External hydraulic power source for applications where hydraulics are a better option than electricity.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A 48 V motor-pump set with a small tank and valve block, with quick couplings at the front and rear for hydraulic implements. Useful for high force at slow speed where linear actuators are too weak.

## Reuse from existing implements

Motor and mounting from the heavy-duty motors idea; hydraulics from the irrigation reel (which has its own).

## Open questions

- Flow and pressure class
- Tank size and cooling
- Coupling standard for implements
