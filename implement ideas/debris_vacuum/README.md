# Debris vacuum

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Transport & General.

## What it does

Sucks up litter and debris from grassland: trash after a festival, or shards of solar panels that a fire on a nearby roof spread over the fields. Several passes clean up the mess.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A wide suction nozzle close to the grass with a fan, a cyclone or bag and a collecting bin on the robot. Glass and panel shards are heavy and sharp, so the nozzle and bin need wear protection; a rotating brush in the nozzle lifts pieces out of the sward.

## Reuse from existing implements

Bin on the upper beams of the dumper barrow or compost spreader; brush and lift of the broom sweeper.

## Open questions

- Fan power and air flow on 48 V
- Nozzle width and height above the grass
- Separation of debris and grass, dust filtering
- Wear protection against glass shards
