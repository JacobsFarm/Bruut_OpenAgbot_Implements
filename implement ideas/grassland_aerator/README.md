# Grassland aerator

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Soil Cultivation & Weed Control.

## What it does

Improving soil structure and air supply in grassland.

## Concept (first thoughts)

Spiked or hollow-tine rollers, or a row of spring-loaded slitting knives, behind or in front of the robot. The element presses down with robot weight and ballast; a rolling drum carries the tines so no extra drive is needed.

## Reuse from existing implements

Floating lift frame with gas springs of the overseeder (the aerator can be combined with it as a slot cutter); lifted by the same actuator.

## Open questions

- Tine type: solid spike, hollow tine or slitting knife
- Down force needed for penetration depth
- Passive roller versus driven roller
- Combining with the overseeder in one pass
