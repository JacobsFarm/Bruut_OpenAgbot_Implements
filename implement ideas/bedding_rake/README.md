# Bedding rake

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Livestock & Farm.

## What it does

Rakes and levels bedding (straw, sawdust, wood chips) in loose housing, stables and paddocks.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A comb or spring-tine harrow behind the robot to loosen and level, with a small hopper to top up. Works with the bedding spreaders.

## Reuse from existing implements

Tine bars and belt of the belt rake; hopper of the sawdust spreader.

## Open questions

- Tine type for straw versus sawdust
- Depth setting on soft bedding
- Animal interaction: noise and speed near animals
