# Feed mixing and distribution wagon

**Status: idea, not worked out yet.** Category: Livestock & Farm.

## What it does

Loads, weighs and mixes a ration and lays it along the feed fence several times a day. Seen on the Lely Vector, DeLaval OptiWagon, GEA DairyFeed F4500, Kuhn Aura and Trioliet Triomatic.

## Concept (first thoughts)

A small mixing bin with a vertical auger on the upper beams and a discharge door or cross conveyor at the side. Loads at a feed kitchen (loaded by hand or from a hopper), weighs on load cells and drives along the feed fence on the same route as the feed pusher.

## Reuse from existing implements

Auger and side discharge of the feed pusher auger; load cell weighing of the mobile refiller; route along the feed fence of the feed pusher.

## Open questions

- Bin volume versus mass and stability
- Mixing power of a vertical auger on a battery
- Loading: feed kitchen, hopper or by hand
- Feeding small groups (calves, young stock) versus the whole herd
