# Flail mulcher - rationale

## Under the belly

A flail mulcher is heavy (rotor, housing, roller: ~100 kg). Behind the robot in the three-point hitch it would lift the
front wheels off when raised (the rotary tiller needs 92 kg ballast). Between the axles the mass adds to all four
wheels (front share 56 %), and the robot keeps steering. The space between the tyres (x +-300, y +-262, 29 mm to a
front tyre at 30 degrees) gives 548 mm of rotor: one strip per pass between rows, or the alley of a narrow orchard.
A swing-out side section for under the tree row (the idea's option) is left for a next variant.

## Flails, speed and cutting height

Y-knives on shackles swing back on stones and wood instead of breaking. 45 m/s at the tips is the usual range for
grass and prunings; the motor (3000 rpm) drives the rotor through a 90/100 poly-V belt to 2700 rpm. 4 staggered rows
give 360 cuts per metre at 0.5 m/s. The rear roller sets the cutting height (40 mm) and presses the mulch down; skids
at the front carry the housing over bumps.

## Power and battery

Mulching energy per area (1 kJ/m2 grass, 2 residues, 4 prunings, estimated) plus ~350 W rotor windage. Grass at
0.8 m/s needs ~1.1 kW in all (1.5 h per charge); prunings use the full 2 kW motor rating at 0.7 m/s (0.7 h). For a
day in an orchard, a battery swap or the hybrid range extender is needed.

## Lift

Two 1500 N actuators beside the rails lift 99 kg (factor 3). Guide posts in sleeves keep the housing level; in work
the actuators are extended with slack in their slotted top clevis, so the housing floats on the roller. The motor sits
between the rails so that it passes under them lifted (6 mm).

## Stone throw

At 45 m/s a flail throws stones far: the housing is closed on all sides, with a rubber flap at the front and the
roller at the rear; the mulcher only runs with the hood down (lift sensor) and stops when lifted.

## Estimated, not measured

- Mulching energy per material (1 / 2 / 4 kJ/m2) and rotor windage (350 W)
- Motor rating (2 kW), mass (11 kg), belt efficiency
- Actuator force (1500 N) and mass
- Drive power while mulching (200 W)
